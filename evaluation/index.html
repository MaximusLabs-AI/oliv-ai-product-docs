Overview#

What the status buttons mean#

Source-backed = described in the 9 October source assessment; Partial = incomplete evidence; Needs confirmation = no evidence established; Reported no = dated assessment of missing proof; By design = one ambiguous CRM prerequisite row.

Claimed in sources does not mean approved to claim publicly. All 555 Oliv owner-decision cells were blank in the supplied buyer map. Production availability and public-claim approval remain unverified.

Important exceptions#

TR2, TR7 and TR9 are historical proof-visibility gaps. P2 has ambiguous criterion polarity and needs owner interpretation. Do not read these four rows as four missing product features.

Source and review dates#

Buyer and AI assessment baseline: 9 October 2026. Documentation source review: 16 October 2026. The workbench preserves the source date instead of silently upgrading historical evidence.
