## Overview

## Who owns the outcome

Onboarding leaders, implementation teams, CSMs and account owners are the principal stakeholders for this domain. Set a concrete outcome and name the owner of each handoff.

## Oliv’s described role

Relevant named agents: Handoff Hank and Onboarding Tracker. Current availability, plan inclusion and data access require confirmation for the customer workspace.

## What a useful pilot proves

Choose a representative workflow with both a normal and an exception case. Record the original source context, the proposed action, its reviewer and the external-system result. Judge the workflow on observed behavior, not the number of agent names shown.
