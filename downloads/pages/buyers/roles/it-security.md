# IT, Security and Legal evaluation guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

A documented data boundary, least-privilege access and current security evidence.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Approval gate and risk reviewer. Approve access, review security and privacy evidence and validate revocation and lifecycle controls.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

A documented data boundary, least-privilege access and current security evidence.

Unknown data use; excessive connector scopes; unclear retention and auditability.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Security; governance; integrations. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Security and privacy](../../administration/security-and-privacy.md)
- [Security and administration reference](../../features/security.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.
