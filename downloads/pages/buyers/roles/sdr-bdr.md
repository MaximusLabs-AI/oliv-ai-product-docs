## Overview

Prioritized research and appropriate outreach with clear qualification and handoff.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Daily prospecting user. Validate ICP and contact quality, review sending behavior and hand off qualified demand.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

Prioritized research and appropriate outreach with clear qualification and handoff.

Thin account coverage; disconnected enrichment; slow inbound response.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Prospecting; sequencing; qualification. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Prospector agent](../../agents/prospector.md)
- [Sales engagement and prospecting reference](../../features/sales-engagement.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.
