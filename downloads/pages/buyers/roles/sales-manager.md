# Sales Manager evaluation guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Useful pipeline reviews and evidence-based coaching between recurring 1:1s.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Team champion and daily reviewer. Review risk and coaching evidence, approve rep goals and set a practical review cadence.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

Useful pipeline reviews and evidence-based coaching between recurring 1:1s.

Status meetings consume coaching time; qualification gaps discovered late.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Deal execution; coaching; meeting follow-through. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Coach agent](../../agents/coach.md)
- [Coaching and enablement reference](../../features/coaching.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.

<a id="measurement-plan"></a>
## Measurement plan

Choose a baseline and fixed pilot cohort. These are evaluation measures, not promised improvements.

- Preparation and review time, measured in the pilot
- Owned next-step completion
- Coaching goal progress with call evidence
- Risk interventions and correction quality

<a id="evaluation-risks"></a>
## Risks to inspect



- Coaching becoming a score without context
- Notifications replacing a useful manager conversation
- Access to another manager’s restricted team data

<a id="decision-questions"></a>
## Decision questions



- Why did this deal or rep receive this recommendation?
- Can I edit or reject the proposed coaching goal?
- What changes after a rep or account changes owner?

<a id="committee-handoff"></a>
## Buying committee handoff

The business sponsor approves the outcome and budget. RevOps owns definitions and mappings; source administrators approve access; managers and daily users review quality; Security, Legal and Finance close their approval gates. One person may hold several roles in a smaller team.
