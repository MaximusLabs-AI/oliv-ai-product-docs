# Chief Revenue Officer evaluation guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Forecast confidence, pipeline quality and aligned sales-to-CS execution.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Economic buyer. Define revenue outcomes and an acceptable operating risk; align VP Sales, RevOps and CS ownership.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

Forecast confidence, pipeline quality and aligned sales-to-CS execution.

Repeated missed forecasts; inconsistent board reporting; unclear account risk.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Forecasting; revenue intelligence; customer success. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Prepare and review a forecast](../../workflows/forecast-review.md)
- [Forecasting reference](../../features/forecasting.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.

<a id="measurement-plan"></a>
## Measurement plan

Choose a baseline and fixed pilot cohort. These are evaluation measures, not promised improvements.

- Forecast versus actual by period
- Pipeline coverage and movement with agreed definitions
- Renewal and expansion exposure without double counting
- Human review effort and accountability

<a id="evaluation-risks"></a>
## Risks to inspect



- Overconfident forecasts from incomplete evidence
- Separate sales and CS interpretations of the same account
- Cost savings claims without a real baseline

<a id="decision-questions"></a>
## Decision questions



- What changed between the submitted forecast and actuals?
- Which source supports a category or value change?
- Which risks require executive intervention and who owns them?

<a id="committee-handoff"></a>
## Buying committee handoff

The business sponsor approves the outcome and budget. RevOps owns definitions and mappings; source administrators approve access; managers and daily users review quality; Security, Legal and Finance close their approval gates. One person may hold several roles in a smaller team.
