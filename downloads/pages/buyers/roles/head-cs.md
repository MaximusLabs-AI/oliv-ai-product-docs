# Head of Customer Success evaluation guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Clear renewal exposure, account priorities and an accountable expansion handoff.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Post-sale sponsor. Define health evidence, renewal metrics, portfolio owners and the CS-to-sales handoff.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

Clear renewal exposure, account priorities and an accountable expansion handoff.

Late churn surprises; fragmented account context; expansion signals lost between teams.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Customer health; renewal forecasting; expansion. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Portfolio Manager agent](../../agents/portfolio-manager.md)
- [Customer success and expansion reference](../../features/customer-success.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.

<a id="measurement-plan"></a>
## Measurement plan

Choose a baseline and fixed pilot cohort. These are evaluation measures, not promised improvements.

- Renewal coverage and explained risk
- Account-health evidence freshness
- CS-to-sales handoff ownership
- NRR and expansion definitions with agreed attribution

<a id="evaluation-risks"></a>
## Risks to inspect



- Treating conversation sentiment as complete health
- Missing or unmapped product usage
- Expansion automation conflicting with the customer relationship

<a id="decision-questions"></a>
## Decision questions



- Which inputs drive this account’s risk flag?
- Which renewals and expansion amounts are included?
- Who approves a warm introduction and CRM opportunity?

<a id="committee-handoff"></a>
## Buying committee handoff

The business sponsor approves the outcome and budget. RevOps owns definitions and mappings; source administrators approve access; managers and daily users review quality; Security, Legal and Finance close their approval gates. One person may hold several roles in a smaller team.
