# Account Manager evaluation guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Relationship continuity, clear account priorities and timely expansion opportunities.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Commercial owner of existing accounts. Own commercial next steps, validate expansion evidence and coordinate with CS.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

Relationship continuity, clear account priorities and timely expansion opportunities.

Stakeholder changes; weak whitespace visibility; inconsistent QBR preparation.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Account planning; relationships; expansion. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Account Management](../../teams/account-management.md)
- [Customer success and expansion reference](../../features/customer-success.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.
