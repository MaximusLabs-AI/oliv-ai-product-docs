# VP of Sales evaluation guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Repeatable deal execution and manager visibility without rebuilding every status report.

<a id="role"></a>
## Role in the buying committee

Executive sponsor and sales operating owner. Choose a sales pilot team, define stage discipline and sponsor manager adoption.

<a id="jobs"></a>
## Jobs and pain signals

Repeatable deal execution and manager visibility without rebuilding every status report.

Inconsistent methodology; stale pipeline; uneven rep performance.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Where to start

Priority evaluation areas: Revenue intelligence; coaching; CRM automation. Start with one workflow and agree the people, data and decision rights needed to evaluate it.

- [Run a pipeline review](../../workflows/pipeline-review.md)
- [Revenue intelligence reference](../../features/revenue-intelligence.md)

<a id="success"></a>
## Evidence to request



- A representative result from your own permitted account and interaction data.
- The original source behind the recommendation, summary or proposed write.
- An exception case showing what happens when information is missing or contradictory.
- A dated statement of scope, availability, costs and operational ownership.

<a id="handoffs"></a>
## Handoffs and adoption

Agree who can configure the workflow, who reviews its output and who owns the downstream action. A successful demonstration does not establish that every team can use it without permissions, data preparation or training.

<a id="measurement-plan"></a>
## Measurement plan

Choose a baseline and fixed pilot cohort. These are evaluation measures, not promised improvements.

- Pipeline review preparation and action completion
- Methodology and qualification completeness
- Rep and manager adoption
- Win/loss and stage progression, with attribution limits

<a id="evaluation-risks"></a>
## Risks to inspect



- Automating an inconsistent sales process
- Alert volume that obscures important changes
- Assuming every sales role has the same coaching support

<a id="decision-questions"></a>
## Decision questions



- Which deal gaps are visible earlier than in the existing review?
- What remains the rep’s and manager’s responsibility?
- How are rejected or inaccurate suggestions corrected?

<a id="committee-handoff"></a>
## Buying committee handoff

The business sponsor approves the outcome and budget. RevOps owns definitions and mappings; source administrators approve access; managers and daily users review quality; Security, Legal and Finance close their approval gates. One person may hold several roles in a smaller team.
