# Growth stage planning guide

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Growing teams need consistent process without adding administrative effort at every handoff.

<a id="needs"></a>
## Operating needs

Growing teams need consistent process without adding administrative effort at every handoff.

Resolve stage and field definitions, then expand through manager-led teams.

<a id="pilot"></a>
## First pilot

A manager pipeline review with CRM proposals and meeting follow-through.

1. Name the business sponsor, operational owner and daily reviewers.
2. Connect only the systems needed for the first workflow.
3. Define a normal case and an exception case, with observable acceptance criteria.
4. Review quality, time spent reviewing, unresolved errors and actual usage cost before expanding.

<a id="readiness"></a>
## Readiness checks



- CRM ownership and clean account/opportunity identities.
- Permitted capture and access to sufficient interaction history.
- A workable approval cadence and escalation route.
- An approved commercial quote for the intended user and activity mix.

<a id="open"></a>
## Qualification to confirm

Confirm minimum or maximum supported team size, contract commitment, implementation service, required seats and any segment-specific restrictions. Historical ICP bands are not hard exclusions.
