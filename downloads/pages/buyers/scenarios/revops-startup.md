# Head of Revenue Operations in smb and startup

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Reliable CRM data, consistent definitions, auditable automation and manageable administration. Apply this role’s priorities to smb and startup operating constraints.

<a id="decision"></a>
## Decision context

Own field mappings, process definitions, connector access and approval policy.

Founder-led or small revenue teams need fast, focused adoption with clear ownership.

<a id="pain"></a>
## Why the buyer evaluates now

Manual cleanup; disconnected tools; conflicting reporting; opaque AI writes.

Avoid a broad configuration project before proving one workflow. Assign a CRM owner even when operations is part-time.

<a id="scope"></a>
## Bound the pilot

A recording-to-follow-up workflow or a small prospecting cohort.

Evaluate crm automation; governance; integrations; analytics using the corresponding buyer criteria rather than a generic feature demonstration.

- [Oliver agent](../../agents/oliver.md)
- [CRM automation and quality reference](../../features/crm-automation.md)

<a id="accept"></a>
## Evidence and acceptance



1. Agree the baseline and intended outcome with the sponsor.
2. Choose representative records and reviewers for this stage.
3. Run one full workflow including an exception and a denied or deferred action.
4. Inspect source evidence, human intervention and downstream state.
5. Approve expansion only after quality, effort, ownership and cost have been reviewed.

<a id="tradeoffs"></a>
## Tradeoffs and open questions

Confirm which work remains with the buyer’s existing systems and people. Check implementation effort, role availability, security requirements and the commercial model for this scenario.
