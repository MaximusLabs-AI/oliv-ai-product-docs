# Company-stage and ICP planning

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Use the master’s company-stage context to plan scope and adoption without treating historical size bands as eligibility rules.

<a id="bands"></a>
## Historical segment bands

The master includes SMB/startup, growth and mid-market scenarios based on employees, ARR and rep counts. Other supplied ICP notes use different cutoffs. This guide retains the operating differences and leaves exact qualification thresholds for Oliv confirmation.

<a id="enterprise"></a>
## Enterprise context

Enterprise requirements are included as evaluation requirements. This does not assert enterprise entitlements, deployment options or service levels that have not been verified.

<a id="source-segment-models"></a>
## Historical segment models to reconcile

These are source planning bands, not verified purchasing thresholds. The current startup offer and older ICP exclusions need explicit commercial reconciliation.

| Master planning view | Historical band | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company-profile SMB | 5–25 revenue reps | Small-team ownership and focused adoption; not an eligibility rule |
| Company-profile mid-market | 25–100/200 revenue reps | Growing process complexity; boundaries are inconsistent in source |
| Company-profile enterprise | 100–500+ revenue reps | Multi-team governance and procurement requirements |
| Question universe SMB/startup | 10–100 employees; under $10M ARR | Research context for the original questions |
| Question universe growth | 50–200 employees; $10M–$50M ARR | Research context; overlaps other source bands |
| Question universe mid-market | 200–1,000 employees; $50M–$150M ARR | Research context; not a product entitlement |
