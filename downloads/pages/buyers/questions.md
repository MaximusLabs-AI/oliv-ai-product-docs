<a id="hypothetical"></a>
## Questions are research prompts

These are buyer-intent and research questions from the supplied master. Their premises, competitor descriptions, price examples, numeric outcomes and proposed capabilities have not been verified. They must not be quoted as current product facts.

The bank preserves all Q1–Q735 records. Source subsection range labels sometimes overlap; each row’s own ID is used for traceability. Related documentation provides context and does not imply a fully verified answer to every question.

<a id="answer-policy"></a>
## How to close a question

Open the relevant capability reference, identify the missing factual requirement and obtain source evidence for the exact behavior and scope. Record who verified it and when. Avoid turning a hypothetical question into an asserted answer.
