# Feature and capability reference

*V1 review draft · 2026-10-16 · Source-described scope; confirm workspace availability*

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Explore every one of the 555 buyer evaluation criteria, organized into 17 capability families and 57 groups. Evidence and approval remain separate.

<a id="how-to-read"></a>
## How to read the feature reference

The reference preserves the complete 9 October 2026 buyer map. Each criterion has a stable ID, a buyer question, terminology, evidence disposition and an acceptance check. Gate and High criteria have individual reference pages; all other criteria have full sections in their group guides.

“Evidenced” means described in the assessed sources. It does not mean production-tested, generally available or approved for a public claim. Unknown requirements remain evaluation requirements, not promised Oliv features.
